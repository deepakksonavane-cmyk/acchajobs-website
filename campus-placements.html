Campus Recruitment

Sourcing, engaging and hiring young talent.

Campus recruitment is a strategy for sourcing, engaging and hiring young talent for internship and entry-level positions. College recruiting is typically a tactic for medium- to large-sized companies with high-volume recruiting needs, but can range from small efforts — like working with university career centers — to large-scale operations, like visiting a wide array of colleges throughout the spring and fall semester.

Campus recruitment often involves working with university career services centers and attending career fairs to meet in-person with college students and recent graduates. Finance, technology, business consulting, manufacturing and engineering are a few of the industries that participate most.
