Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is registering with Accha Jobs really free?

Yes. Free Placement lets you register your profile at no cost — we match you against live company requirements as they come in. Premium Placement is an optional paid upgrade for unlimited calls and priority updates.

How long does it take to get placed?

It depends on your role, experience and current openings in our active pipeline. Premium members get daily updates and faster follow-up, which typically shortens the timeline.

Do you only place freshers, or experienced candidates too?

Both. We work with freshers through campus drives and our College to Corporate program, and with experienced candidates across technical, banking, industrial and corporate roles.

Does Accha Jobs charge companies for hiring?

We offer zero-cost hiring assistance for companies — sourcing, screening and coordination handled end-to-end, so you pay only for results.

How do I get started?

Fill the form on our For Job Seekers or For Employers page — it opens directly in WhatsApp with your details pre-filled, and our team gets back to you from there.
